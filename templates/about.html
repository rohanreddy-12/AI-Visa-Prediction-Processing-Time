The H-1B Journey

Understand the timeline our model predicts.

Step 1: LCA Filing

Labor Condition Application

Employer submits ETA Form 9035 to the Department of Labor to ensure wage compliance. (Takes ~7 days).

Step 2: Petition Filing

USCIS Form I-129

Once LCA is approved, the employer files the petition with USCIS. This is where our AI Estimator begins its calculation.

Step 3: Processing

USCIS Adjudication

The core waiting period. Time varies wildly based on service center, wage level, and SOC code (Usually 2-6 months).

Step 4: Decision

Approval or RFE

USCIS issues an Approval Notice (I-797) or a Request for Evidence (RFE) which extends the timeline.
